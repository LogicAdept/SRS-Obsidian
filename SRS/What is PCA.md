**What is PCA?**

Linear projection onto directions of max variance (eigenvectors of covariance / SVD). Use for compression, visualization, noise. Fit on train only. Not supervised (doesn't use y) — for discriminative reduction see LDA or supervised embeddings. Assumes linear correlations.
