**What is ClickHouse?**

Column-oriented OLAP DBMS (Yandex origin). Batch inserts, huge scans/aggregates, sparse indexes, weak point-updates/transactions. Not Postgres. Interview: OLAP dashboards/logs/events, not bookings/balances.
