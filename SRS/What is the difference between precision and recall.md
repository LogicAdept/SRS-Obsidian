**Precision vs recall vs F1?**

Precision = TP/(TP+FP): of predicted positives, how many are right. Recall = TP/(TP+FN): of actual positives, how many you caught. F1 = harmonic mean. High precision when FP is costly (spam, moderation). High recall when FN is costly (cancer, fraud catch). Threshold moves the tradeoff.
