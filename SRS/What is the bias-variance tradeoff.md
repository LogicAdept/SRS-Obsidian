**Explain the bias-variance tradeoff.**

Bias: error from wrong assumptions (underfit). Variance: sensitivity to the sample (overfit). Total error ≈ bias² + variance + noise. Regularization, more data, and model class move the levers. Interview: say how you'd tell which one you have from curves.
