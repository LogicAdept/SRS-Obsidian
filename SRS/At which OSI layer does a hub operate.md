Hubs are Layer 1 (Physical) devices in interview taxonomy. They repeat electrical signals to all ports with no MAC learning. Switches = Layer 2; routers = Layer 3.
> [!warning] Unverified traps from the dump
> - Hubs create a single collision domain; that property is why they disappeared from modern Ethernet designs.
