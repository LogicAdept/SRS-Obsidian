**How would you start a recommender?**

Collaborative: similar users/items from interaction matrix (cold start problem). Content-based: item attributes/text. Hybrid common. Cold start: popularity, content, explore. Evaluate ranking (nDCG, recall@k), not only accuracy. Business objective first.
