**What is Kafka Connect?**

Connect: source/sink connectors, distributed workers, offset tracking in Kafka, standard ETL (JDBC, S3, Debezium). Custom consumer: business logic, branching, non-standard sinks.
Interviewers want: I do not write a consumer to dump Postgres into S3 if a connector exists.
