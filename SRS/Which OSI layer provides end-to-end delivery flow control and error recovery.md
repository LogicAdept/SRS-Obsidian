> [!warning] Untrusted draft
> Copied from an external question dump. Not checked against official documentation. Do not treat this as a review answer.

The Transport layer (Layer 4) provides end-to-end communication between hosts. Interview dumps associate it with segmentation, flow control, and error recovery (especially TCP). UDP is also Layer 4 but without TCP-style reliability.
> [!warning] Unverified traps from the dump
> - End-to-end reliability is not guaranteed for every Layer 4 protocol — UDP is connectionless and unreliable by design.
