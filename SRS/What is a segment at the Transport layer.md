In OSI/TCP teaching dumps, a segment is the Transport-layer PDU, especially for TCP: application data plus transport header (ports, sequence/ack fields, etc.). UDP payloads are often called datagrams instead.
> [!warning] Unverified traps from the dump
> - Using segment for UDP is sloppy but common in multiple-choice keys that force one Layer-4 PDU name.
