> [!warning] Untrusted draft
> Copied from an external question dump. Not checked against official documentation. Do not treat this as a review answer.

The Physical layer (Layer 1) converts between data bits and signals on the medium (electrical, optical, radio). It covers cables, connectors, voltages, timing, and other media characteristics. Hubs and repeaters are classic Layer 1 devices in interview lists.
> [!warning] Unverified traps from the dump
> - Physical layer does not perform MAC learning or IP routing; those belong above Layer 1.
