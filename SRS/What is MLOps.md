**What is MLOps?**

CI/CD for models: version data/code/models, reproducible training, registry, automated tests, canary deploy, monitoring, rollback. Feature store for train/serve consistency. Not just 'pickle a sklearn model'. MLflow/Kubeflow/cloud pipelines are tools, not the definition.
