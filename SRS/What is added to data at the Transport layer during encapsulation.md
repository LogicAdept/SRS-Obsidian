At the Transport layer, dumps say a header with source and destination port numbers is added, producing a segment (TCP wording). IP addresses are added later at the Network layer; MAC addresses at the Data Link layer.
> [!warning] Unverified traps from the dump
> - Port numbers are not MAC or IP addresses — mixing the three headers is a classic wrong answer.
