**Why is `Map` not a `Collection`, while `List` and `Set` are?**

- `Collection` is a group of elements.
- `Map` is a group of key–value pairs, not a collection of lone elements.
- Same prompt also appears as “why is Map modeled separately” and “why is Map both a data structure and a Collections Framework type.”
