#Networking/OSI #SRS #New

> [!warning] Untrusted draft
> Copied from an external question dump. Not checked against official documentation. Do not treat this as a review answer.

Bottom-up troubleshooting starts at Layer 1 and moves upward. After confirming physical connectivity (cables, link lights, media), the next check is typically Data Link (MAC addressing, switching, local frames), then Network (IP), then Transport and Application services.
> [!warning] Unverified traps from the dump
> - Top-down and divide-and-conquer are valid alternatives; dumps expect you to know the next layer after Physical is Data Link.
