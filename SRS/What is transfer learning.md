**What is transfer learning?**

Reuse a model pretrained on a large dataset; freeze early layers or fine-tune on your task. Works when target data is small. Domain shift still hurts. In NLP/vision this is the default; in 2026 often 'start from a foundation model' not ImageNet from scratch.
