> [!warning] Untrusted draft
> Copied from an external question dump. Not checked against official documentation. Do not treat this as a review answer.

A packet is the Network layer PDU. It carries logical addressing (IP) used for routing between networks. Routers forward packets; they do not primarily forward by MAC the way L2 switches do.
> [!warning] Unverified traps from the dump
> - Some texts say datagram instead of packet for IP; interviews usually accept packet for Layer 3 PDU.
