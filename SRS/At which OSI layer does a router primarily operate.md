Routers primarily operate at Layer 3 (Network). They use logical addresses (IP) to forward packets between different networks.
> [!warning] Unverified traps from the dump
> - Modern routers also terminate higher-layer features (NAT, ACLs inspecting ports), but the primary OSI mapping in interviews remains Layer 3.
