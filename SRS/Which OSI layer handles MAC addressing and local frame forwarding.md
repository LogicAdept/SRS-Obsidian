The Data Link layer (Layer 2) provides node-to-node transfer on a local link, uses physical/MAC addressing, and deals in frames. Within a LAN segment, switches forward based on MAC addresses at this layer.

> - ARP sits at the Layer 2/3 boundary in many teaching materials even though it resolves IP to MAC.
