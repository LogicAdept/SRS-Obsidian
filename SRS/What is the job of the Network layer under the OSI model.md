#Networking/OSI #SRS #New

> [!warning] Untrusted draft
> Copied from an external question dump. Not checked against official documentation. Do not treat this as a review answer.

Under OSI, the Network layer is responsible for routing, packet switching, and (in many dumps) congestion-related control across networks. Logical addressing and path selection live here; routers operate primarily at this layer.
> [!warning] Unverified traps from the dump
> - Congestion control also appears at Transport (TCP); dumps sometimes over-attribute it to Network.
