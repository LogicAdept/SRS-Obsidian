**Supervised vs unsupervised vs RL?**

Supervised: labeled (x, y) — fraud, prices. Unsupervised: structure in unlabeled data — clustering, PCA, segmentation. Reinforcement: agent, reward, sequential decisions — ads bidding, games. Semi-supervised and self-supervised sit in between (pretrain then fine-tune).
