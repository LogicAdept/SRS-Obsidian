**What is a Random Forest?**

Bagging of deep trees + random feature subset at each split (decorrelates trees). Strong tabular baseline, fewer knobs than boosting, parallel train. Weaker than well-tuned GBDT on many tables. Feature randomness is why it's not just bagged trees.
